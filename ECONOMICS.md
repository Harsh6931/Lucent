# ECONOMICS

## 1. Converted Lead Value to Credex
When a startup lead is captured, they have identified potential SaaS waste. We assume the following for an average startup:
- **Average Startup SaaS Spend Audited**: $2,000/month ($24,000/year).
- **Average Computed Savings (20%)**: $400/month ($4,800/year).
- **Credex Business Model**: Credex charges a percentage of savings realized (25% fee on savings) or earns a margin on discounted bulk credit purchases.
- **Estimated Annual Revenue Per Customer**: $1,200/year (25% of $4,800).
- **Average Customer Retention**: 1.25 years (typical early-stage startup runway cycles).
- **Estimated Lifetime Value (LTV)**: **$1,500** ($1,200 * 1.25).

## 2. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) by Channel
Since we are using a $0 paid budget, CAC is measured in opportunity cost of founder time valued at $50/hour:
- **Direct LinkedIn/X Outreach**: 2 hours of outreach/founder time per converted customer. **CAC = $100**.
- **Reddit / Hacker News Content Marketing**: 4 hours to write a high-value post, yielding 20 completed audits. With a 5% booking rate and 30% close rate, 20 audits yield 0.3 customers. **CAC = (4 * $50) / 0.3 = $666** (initial, but drops as posts remain searchable index items).
- **Viral Scorecard Sharing**: User-driven sharing costs $0 in developer/founder time. **CAC = $0**.
- **Open-Source GitHub Action Integration**: 10 hours of initial development time ($500). Over 6 months, it yields 100 audits/month, producing 1.5 customers/month. **CAC = $500 / (1.5 * 6) = $55**.

## 3. Required Conversion Rates
To maintain a healthy, profitable funnel, we track two primary conversion ratios:
- **Audit Completed -> Consultation Booked (Handoff Rate)**: **5.0%**
  - High-savings audits (> $500/mo savings): 10.0%
  - Medium-savings audits ($100–$500/mo savings): 3.0%
  - Low/No-savings audits: 0.5%
- **Consultation Booked -> Credit Purchase (Close Rate)**: **30.0%**
  - High-intent founders booking consultations after seeing clear numeric savings close at a high rate.

## 4. Conditions Needed to Reach $1M ARR in 18 Months
To hit $1M ARR ($83,333/month in revenue):
- **Active Paying Customers Required**: **833 active startups** ($83,333 / $100/mo average revenue).
- **Total Booked Consultations Needed**: **2,778** ($833 / 30% close rate).
- **Total Completed Audits Needed**: **55,560** ($2,778 / 5% handoff conversion rate).
- **Average Audits Per Month**: **3,087 audits/month** (approx. 100 audits/day).
- **Growth Trigger**: Requires the viral share coefficient to exceed 0.15 (meaning for every 10 audits run, 1.5 public links are shared, drawing in more organic traffic).
