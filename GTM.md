# GTM

## 1. Exact Target User
- **Specific Title**: Technical Founders, CTOs, and Engineering Managers (EMs).
- **Company Stage**: Early-stage startups (Seed to Series A, team sizes of 5 to 50 members) experiencing rapid growth in engineering stacks.
- **Problem**: Wasted software expenditure due to overlapping AI tool licenses (e.g., developers individually subscribing to Cursor while the company pays for GitHub Copilot seats), over-tiered team plans, and unmonitored pay-as-you-go developer API keys. They need quick spend audits to extend runway without procurement overhead.

## 2. Pre-Need Search/Reading Intent
Before needing this tool, target users search for:
- *"Cursor vs Copilot pricing"*
- *"OpenAI API billing explanation"*
- *"Reduce Claude API costs"*
- *"Reduce startup SaaS spend"*
- *"Best coding assistant pricing for teams"*
- *"Supabase vs Firebase pricing comparison"*

## 3. Specific Online Communities & Channels
We will reach users where they hang out:
- **Reddit**: `/r/startups`, `/r/saas`, `/r/nextjs`, `/r/cto`, and `/r/ExperiencedDevs`.
- **Hacker News**: Show HN launches and discussion threads regarding AI development assistants (Cursor/Windsurf) or API billing.
- **Twitter/X**: Developer-founder circles using the `#buildinpublic` hashtag, and communities surrounding Next.js, Vercel, Supabase, and Claude.
- **Slack/Discord**: Y Combinator Startup School Slack, Launch House Discord, and localized tech founder meetups.

## 4. 30-Day $0 Budget Traction Strategy (First 100 Users)
- **Days 1-10 (Direct Outreach & Beta Feedback)**: Reach out directly to 50 startup founders in our personal networks or via LinkedIn/X matching the target profile. Frame the pitch: *"I built a free tool to calculate if your dev team is overpaying for AI licenses. Would love your quick feedback on the recommendation engine."*
- **Days 11-20 (Community Value Drops)**: Publish a text-heavy, high-value post on `/r/startups` and `/r/saas` detailing *"How our dev team accidentally spent $1.5k on AI APIs and how to prevent it."* Insert a link to Lucent as a free tool to check spend.
- **Days 21-30 (Hacker News Launch)**: Submit a structured Show HN launch: *"Show HN: Lucent - Check if your startup dev team is overpaying for Cursor, Copilot, and APIs."* Follow up with comments explaining our deterministic evaluation policy.

## 5. The Unfair Distribution Channel
- **Viral Social Share Loop**: The dynamic social media metadata (`/r/[publicId]`) allows founders to share their "SaaS audit scorecard" on LinkedIn/Twitter/X to show off how much money they saved or to share with other founders. Sharing this scorecard provides organic viral loop expansion.
- **Open-Source GitHub Integration**: Publish a GitHub Action that checks `package.json` or team toolings, automatically running an audit as part of a repository CI pipeline, reporting potential savings in a PR comment.

## 6. Week-1 Traction Targets
- **Audits Completed**: 80 audits run.
- **Lead Captures**: 30 emails collected (representing a 37.5% conversion rate).
- **Public Reports Shared**: 10 public scorecards shared.
- **Annualized Savings Found**: $15,000 in aggregate.
