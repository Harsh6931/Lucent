## FAQ (5 real Q&As)

### Q1: How does the audit engine calculate savings?
**A:** Lucent uses a deterministic, rule-based engine mapping against official pricing plans from tools like Cursor, Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, and development APIs. We compare your current seats, reported spend, and use-case to model cheaper tiers, optimal plan fits, or cross-vendor alternatives.

### Q2: Do you share my audit data or email with third parties?
**A:** Never. Lucent values privacy and security. The public report share link (`/r/[publicId]`) strips all personally identifiable information (PII) like email, company name, or role. Only aggregated, PII-safe savings data is rendered in the public view.

### Q3: What if my spend deviates from standard plans due to custom pricing?
**A:** Our engine cross-references reported spend against modeled baseline plans. If a significant mismatch is detected, the engine labels the recommendation as medium confidence and attaches a specific assumption note, indicating that custom discounts or API overages may be present.

### Q4: Is Lucent actually free, or is there a catch?
**A:** Lucent is 100% free to audit your spend. If you discover substantial savings (e.g. >$500/mo), we offer an optional consultation link to discuss credit optimization packages via Credex to further reduce SaaS spend.

### Q5: Can I export or share my results with my team?
**A:** Yes. Once your audit is generated, you can copy the secure, shareable public link. It displays a clean snapshot of your savings recommendations without disclosing any team contact details or personal identifiers.
